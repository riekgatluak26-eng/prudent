Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, pricing, and operations.

Our monthly rates range from USD 250 to USD 750 per guard depending on site risk, location, and specific requirements. All rates include training, uniforms, and supervision. We require a 50% advance payment with clear contract terms.

Every guard completes a 1-week induction covering drill, report writing, customer service, fire safety, and first aid. We also conduct monthly refresher training to keep skills sharp and maintain compliance.

We primarily provide unarmed guards. However, for high-risk sites or cash-in-transit operations, we can arrange for armed security personnel in coordination with relevant authorities and proper licensing.

For urgent requirements, we can deploy a temporary guard within 24 hours in most areas of Juba. Permanent assignments typically begin within 3–5 days after contract signing and site assessment.
